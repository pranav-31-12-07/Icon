Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When do admissions for the 2026–27 session close?

Admissions are conducted on a rolling first-come, first-served basis as class batches have a strictly enforced low teacher-to-student ratio. Early registration is recommended.

Q: Can we meet the Principal and Management before confirming?

Absolutely. In fact, we encourage every family to tour our Jyotinagar campus, inspect the labs, and interact with Shivkumar Niyogi and academic coordinators.

Q: Does the school provide bus or van transportation?

Yes, safe transit options cover Osmanpura, Jyotinagar, Samarth Nagar, and adjacent neighborhoods with experienced drivers and staff attendants.
