About Icon English School

Dedicated to academic rigor, character building, and individual mentorship in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

Our History

A Legacy of Nurturing Confident Young Citizens

Established in Plot No. 37, Jyotinagar, Osmanpura Parisar, Icon English School was founded with a singular purpose: to deliver an authentic, high-quality English medium education accessible to local families without compromising on moral roots.

Under the visionary leadership of Shivkumar Niyogi, our institution has expanded from an ambitious neighborhood school to a respected center of learning. Over the past decade and a half, we have guided more than 1,200 alumni into top secondary institutions, colleges, and professional vocations across Maharashtra and India.

Located peacefully behind Raka's Life Style Club, our campus shields children from bustling urban traffic while maintaining convenient transit connectivity for families throughout Osmanpura, Samarth Nagar, Railway Station Parisar, and surrounding sectors.

Educational Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Learning at Icon

A balanced curriculum blending academic focus, cognitive stimulation, and holistic development.

1

Concept Clarity

Moving away from rote memorization. Teachers explain principles using real-life examples, classroom demonstrations, and interactive visual aids.

2

English Fluency

Daily conversation hours, elocution, debate, and library reading sessions to cultivate fearless articulation and impeccable vocabulary.

3

Moral & Cultural Roots

Instilling respect for elders, civic responsibility, patriotic honor, and Indian cultural traditions through community assemblies and festivals.

4

Physical Vitality

Structured yoga, athletics, cricket, badminton, and outdoor team play ensuring our children grow strong, healthy, and sports-minded.
