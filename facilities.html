Campus Infrastructure & Facilities

A state-of-the-art learning campus created for interactive discovery, safety, and physical wellness.

Learning Spaces

Explore Our Campus Facilities

Every square foot of our Jyotinagar campus is designed to support curious minds, active physical fitness, and joyful English learning.

Academic Facility Pre-Primary to Class 8

Bright & Ventilated Smart Classrooms

Spacious classrooms engineered to maximize natural daylight and gentle cross-ventilation. Equipped with interactive digital screens, ergonomic wooden student desks, and safe, child-friendly interior architecture.

Key Highlights:
  • • Low student-to-teacher ratio (average 25:1) ensuring individual care
  • • Audio-visual multimedia aids for concept comprehension
  • • Ergonomically designed posture-friendly student desks
  • • Clean, sanitized learning environment throughout the day
Technology Facility IT & Logic

Modern Computer & Digital Lab

High-speed networked computer workstations fostering digital literacy, foundational typing, computational logic, and basic programming concepts in a strictly content-filtered, safe digital environment.

Key Highlights:
  • • 1:1 student-to-computer terminal ratio during lab practicals
  • • Foundational curriculum covering MS Office, Paint, and Scratch coding
  • • High-speed fiber connectivity with child-safe firewall security
  • • Certified, patient computer teachers guiding every step
Scientific Inquiry Physics • Chemistry • Biology

Experiential Science & Activity Lab

We believe science cannot be mastered merely from textbooks. Our well-equipped activity lab allows young students to perform hands-on physics demonstrations, chemistry color changes, and biological specimen observations.

Key Highlights:
  • • Compound and optical microscopes for specimen study
  • • Non-hazardous demonstration kits and working models
  • • Strict laboratory safety protocols and first-aid measures
  • • Annual Science & Innovation Exhibition projects
Knowledge Archive 3,000+ Books

Curated Library & Reading Corner

A quiet sanctuary housing thousands of books ranging from classic English literature, illustrated folk tales, scientific encyclopedias, to historical biographies that cultivate an early love for independent reading.

Key Highlights:
  • • Dedicated weekly library reading hour for every class
  • • Special picture-book section for pre-primary learners
  • • Illustrated periodicals, children's magazines, and newspapers
  • • Book review clubs and storytelling sessions
Fitness & Athletics Physical Education

Sports Grounds & Indoor Activity Bay

Physical fitness builds discipline and teamwork. Our students engage in structured athletics, cricket, badminton, carrom, chess, yoga, and gymnastics under trained physical education coaches.

Key Highlights:
  • • Inter-house sports tournaments and annual track athletics meet
  • • Morning meditation and yoga assemblies for mental focus
  • • Indoor mind games (Chess & Carrom championships)
  • • Proper sports gear and trained physical instructor mentorship
Safety & Transit 24x7 CCTV

Campus Security, CCTV & Safe Transit

Located in peaceful Jyotinagar behind Raka's Life Style Club. The campus features comprehensive round-the-clock video surveillance, gated security checks, sanitized clean drinking water, and monitored student bus transit.

Key Highlights:
  • • Continuous CCTV coverage covering campus gates and corridors
  • • Reverse Osmosis (RO) purified drinking water stations
  • • Dedicated campus first-aid infirmary and emergency medical links
  • • Reliable student bus transit connecting Osmanpura Parisar
